Our Legacy of Trust

Serving Kannauj with Purity for 20 Years

Welcome to Verma Jewelers, a trusted name in Chawal Mandi, Kannauj. Founded in 2006, our family-owned business has been built on an unwavering commitment to purity, craftsmanship, and customer trust.

Owned and operated by Mukesh Verma, son of Arvind Verma, we specialize in high-quality 22K gold and pure silver jewelry. From intricate bridal sets to elegant daily wear, every piece is a testament to our rich heritage.
